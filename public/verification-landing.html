    Five modules

    Modules 0–1 are the conceptual on-ramp. Module 2 covers the verification infrastructure itself across hardware, cloud, intelligence and human layers. Module 3 red-teams the whole stack through evasion scenarios. Module 4 asks how to layer imperfect mechanisms into a realistic regime, and ends in the capstone.

    Every module ends in writing

    Most modules ask for one short written output — a memo, brief, critique or design note — applied to a concrete verification problem, and put through peer review. The memo desk collects all fifteen places the outline marks, quotes the brief where one exists, and says plainly where none has been drafted yet.
