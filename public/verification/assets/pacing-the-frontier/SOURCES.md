Every one is a photograph the person publishes of themselves on their own
page. None carries a licence, so `unchecked` is the honest status and the
lesson credits the source rather than claiming a grant. Using them is the
course owner's editorial decision, taken to put a face beside a public
statement its author signed — it is not an inference from terms nobody
published. If any of them objects, delete the file: `Signatory` falls back to
initials with no other change.

Checked first and found nothing usable, so nobody repeats the search:
Wikimedia Commons has no portrait of any of the three; Openverse (which
indexes Flickr's CC pool and Commons) returns nothing for them; Schulman and
Sekhon have English Wikipedia articles with **no image**, and Carroll has no
article. pacingthefrontier.com publishes no signatory photographs.

Ilya Sutskever is not here — his portrait is the Wikimedia Commons one already
committed under `../what-do-they-say/sutskever.jpg`, CC BY / CC BY-SA, and the
block reuses it rather than keeping a second copy.
