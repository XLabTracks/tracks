A countermeasure for exploration hacking is a method that allows us to detect exploration hacking during training, or a method that prevents one of the [necessary conditions for exploration hacking](#ax-sec-conceptual-framework). Different countermeasures are accessible only given certain affordances. For example, some countermeasures only require access to the model's output behavior (black-box measures), whereas other measures require access to model weights and activations (white-box measures), or even the ability to train the model (training-time measures).
