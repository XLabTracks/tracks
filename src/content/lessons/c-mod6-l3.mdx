Congratulations! You have completed the AI Control track made by the Existential Risk Lab from the University of Chicago. Consider the following next steps in your AI Control and broader AI Safety journey.

## AI safety research programs to apply to

- [MATS (ML Alignment & Theory Scholars)](https://matsprogram.org) — the main pipeline into full-time alignment research; Redwood, Apollo, and METR mentors take scholars through it
- [Astra Fellowship (Constellation)](https://www.constellation.org) — research fellowship colocated with Redwood/METR-adjacent researchers in Berkeley
- [ARENA](https://arena.education) — technical upskilling bootcamp (transformers, RL, evals); the standard prep before research programs
- [SPAR (Supervised Program for Alignment Research)](https://sparai.org) — part-time mentored research, remote-friendly; good first research experience
- [LASR Labs](https://www.lasrlabs.org) — London-based mentored research program; control and evals projects have run here
- [Pivotal Research Fellowship](https://www.pivotal-research.org)
- [ERA Fellowship (Cambridge)](https://erafellowship.org) — existential risk research fellowship, technical and governance tracks
- [AI Safety Camp](https://aisafety.camp) — remote, part-time, low barrier to entry
- [XLab Summer Research Fellowship](https://xrisk.uchicago.edu) — our own lab's fellowship at UChicago

## Organizations to track

- [Redwood Research](https://blog.redwoodresearch.org) — the center of gravity for AI control; most of this track's readings came from here
- [Apollo Research](https://www.apolloresearch.ai) — evals, scheming and reward-seeking empirics (the contrastive-SDF paper from this module)
- [METR](https://metr.org) — dangerous-capability and autonomy evals
- [UK AI Security Institute](https://www.aisi.gov.uk) — the largest government AI safety team; control work in their research agenda
- [FAR.AI](https://far.ai) — research and field-building; runs ControlConf and records the talks used in this track
- [Anthropic alignment team](https://alignment.anthropic.com)
- [OpenAI alignment research](https://alignment.openai.com)
