## By the end of Module 1, you will be able to

- Name the major public and private actors relevant to an international AI agreement and locate each on the compute supply chain.
- Characterize each actor’s incentives — to comply, defect, hide, exaggerate, or free-ride — rather than treating actors as a neutral list.

- Produce actor-aware written analysis: a report or briefing that accounts for its context — who produced the underlying information, and who will consume the output.
