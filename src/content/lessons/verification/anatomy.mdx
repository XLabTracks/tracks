Use the three texts to analyze how international agreements translate broad security objectives into concrete obligations, verification arrangements, and procedures for responding to uncertainty or non-compliance.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Task">

Your analysis should:

Compare how the agreements define:

- the regulated actors, objects, and activities;
- prohibited or limited conduct;
- thresholds, exceptions, and jurisdiction;
- declarations, notifications, and record-keeping duties;
- verification rights and evidence sources;
- procedures for clarification, investigation, and dispute resolution;
- and responses to suspected or confirmed violations.

Look for gaps, ambiguities, or design failures across the agreements. Explain why each gap matters. For each gap, specify:

- the failure or evasion pathway it creates;
- which actors could exploit it;
- what evidence would be missing;
- and how the gap could affect confidence, cooperation, or enforcement.

Develop your own view of what an international agreement on advanced AI should contain, and defend the most important trade-offs in your design.
