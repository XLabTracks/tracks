### 2.1.8 Policy judgment: what role should hardware play?

Runtime: 40 minutes Hard idea: A useful recommendation gives hardware a bounded job inside a regime and states the conditions under which that recommendation expires.

#### Source triad

Learners receive three short dossiers representing distinct evidence categories.

##### Dossier 1: Deployed primitive

Official NVIDIA attestation documentation and a sample token.

What learners should notice:

- Actual implementation;
- Concrete evidence fields;
- Working reference-value and revocation services;
- Vendor-centered trust;
- Narrow relation to the treaty’s prohibited-activity claim;
- Lack of automatic topology, workload-history, or completeness assurance.

##### Dossier 2: Empirical component demonstration

The telemetry-classification study.

What learners should notice:

- An adversarially tested empirical component;
- Substantial results under tested conditions;
- Explicit experimental limits;
- Dependency on a trustworthy telemetry path and protected classifier;
- No end-to-end policy or enforcement institution.

##### Dossier 3: Systems proposal

An off-chip monitoring architecture or hardware licensing proposal.

What learners should notice:

- A coherent proposed system;
- Unresolved engineering and operational dependencies;
- Claimed deployment path;
- Possible advantages against vendor or on-chip distrust;
- Absence of deployment evidence at treaty-relevant scale.

This source structure builds the habit of distinguishing:

- Deployed primitive;
- Component demonstration;
- End-to-end prototype;
- Pilot at relevant scale;
- Operating governance regime.

Learners should never infer stage five from evidence at stages one or two.

#### Feasibility stress test

Before writing, each learner receives one change in conditions:

- A major accelerator-signing key is compromised;
- Algorithmic efficiency sharply reduces the compute required for the relevant capability;
- Distributed training becomes less dependent on high-bandwidth co-location;
- A larger share of covered compute shifts to hardware from vendors outside the original trust arrangement;
- A serious physical attack against the measurement path is demonstrated;
- A jointly governed off-chip pilot survives adversarial testing;
- A crisis shortens the available deployment window to three months;
- Parties accept inspections but reject foreign-controlled on-chip keys.

The learner revises:

- Technical feasibility;
- Political feasibility;
- Coverage;
- Fidelity;
- Time to deployment;
- Preferred corroborating layer.

The point is not forecasting the correct future. It is making the assessment conditional on visible facts.

#### Written output: Hardware assurance brief

Length: 700 to 1,000 words, or equivalent structured design note.

Audience: A named national delegation or joint drafting session considering a three-month U.S.-China pause.

Prompt:

Assess the role one proposed hardware architecture should play in verifying a rule that prohibits unlicensed above-threshold training while permitting inference and approved safety evaluations. Recommend a bounded use of the mechanism, a pilot or deployment pathway, and the independent evidence needed to cover its main blind spot.

The brief must contain:

- Goal, rule, and claim State the policy objective, legal obligation, and exact proposition the mechanism tests.
- Actors and authority Name the prover, verifier, evidence producer, key or reference-value authority, regulator, and enforcing institution.
- Evidence and trust chain Explain what is measured, how it reaches the verifier, and which components or actors must remain trustworthy.
- Current maturity and deployment path Separate existing primitives, demonstrated components, proposed components, and missing institutions.
- Strongest adversary response Model the best plausible bypass by the relevant actor.
- Collapsing weakness Rank weaknesses and identify the one that defeats the proposed role if unresolved.
- Residual blind spot and sibling layer State which cloud, intelligence, inspection, or human evidence must corroborate the hardware claim.
- Update condition Name the evidence, trend, or political change that would raise or lower the recommendation.
