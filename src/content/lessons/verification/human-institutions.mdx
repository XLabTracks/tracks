## Lab · 20–24 min

The case file contains deliberately mixed evidence: a partly corroborated
human report, company-selected records, a valid access demand, and a refusal.
Make fourteen decisions in four passes:

1. test the institution’s independence, competence, accountability, authority,
   and actual access;
2. identify financial, informational, cultural, and political capture;
3. separate facts human evidence can establish from facts that require
   technical or physical evidence;
4. choose separately whether the record supports investigation, a compliance
   finding, and enforcement.

Each accepted statement becomes one line of the final institutional judgment.
