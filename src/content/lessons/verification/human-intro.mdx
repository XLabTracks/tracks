<Objectives scope="the core section">
1. **Bound what a source can know.** Say which people across the AI supply chain could observe which activity, where their knowledge stops, and which parts of an account remain inference.
2. **Follow an allegation to evidence.** Trace the path from a report through its reporting channel and protections to material a verifier can act on.
3. **Read a conclusion off its access.** Compare audits and inspections, and state how the access each is granted bounds what it can conclude.
4. **Judge the verifying institution.** Assess whether it is independent and capable, and say when the evidence supports investigation, a compliance finding, or enforcement.
</Objectives>
