## The idea, as posed

From [An International Agreement to Prevent the Premature Creation of
Artificial Superintelligence — Scher, Abecassis, Barnett & Abeyta
(2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10783), Appendix B, What can we do
today? Quoted:

> Establish AI hotlines between key actors in the U.S. and PRC government.

The staged implementation in Appendix C carries the same measure, and says
what it is for:

> These measures build confidence and transparency and reduce the risk of
> misunderstanding rapid AI developments as signs of imminent aggression.
>
> Establish secure communication channels between high-level AI and
> cybersecurity officials in the U.S. and PRC

## What you produce

The design the quoted measure leaves open: what counts as an incident, who
picks up, the escalation ladder, and — the hard part the deliverable names
— why a message on this channel should be believed by a party expecting
deception.
